# Quick Start: Gallery Features

## What's New?

### 📱 In-App Gallery
Your drawings are now automatically saved to an internal gallery that you can access anytime!

### 🎨 Edit Any Drawing
Tap any saved drawing to reopen and continue working on it.

### 🗑️ Manage Your Art
Delete unwanted drawings with a simple tap.

## How to Use

### Save a Drawing
1. Tap the **menu button** (☰) in the top-left
2. Choose **💾 Save to Gallery**
3. Done! Your drawing is saved.

### View Your Gallery
**From Main Menu:**
- Tap **Gallery** button

**While Drawing:**
- Tap menu (☰) → **🖼 My Gallery**

### Edit a Drawing
1. Open the Gallery
2. Tap any drawing card
3. Continue drawing!
4. Save again to update

### Delete a Drawing
1. Open the Gallery
2. Tap the red **×** button on any card
3. Confirm deletion

### Create New Drawing
**From Gallery:**
- Tap the **+** button (top-right)

**While Drawing:**
- Menu (☰) → **📄 New Drawing**

### Export Options
**Save to Gallery** (💾)
- Saves inside the app
- Can edit later
- No permissions needed

**Export to Photos** (📸)
- Saves to iOS Photos
- Needs photo permission
- Static image

**Share** (📤)
- Share via Messages, Mail, etc.
- No permissions needed

## Tips

✅ **Autosave**: Tap "Save to Gallery" frequently while working
✅ **Edit Anytime**: Your saved drawings can always be reopened
✅ **No Limits**: Save as many drawings as you want
✅ **Privacy**: Gallery drawings stay in the app (unless exported)
